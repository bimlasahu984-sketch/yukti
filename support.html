Frequently Asked Questions

How can I open a bank account? +
You can open a bank account online or by visiting your nearest SecureBank branch with valid ID proof and address proof.
What is the minimum balance for Savings Account? +
The minimum balance depends on your account type. Please check account details or contact support.
How can I reset my net banking password? +
Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the verification steps to reset your password.
How can I block my debit/credit card? +
You can instantly block your card using mobile banking, net banking, or by calling customer care.
Where can I find my account statements? +
You can download your account statements from net banking or receive them through email e-statements.
